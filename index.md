## 🎯 What is annona?

Annona manages how your AI agents function. It acts as the brain for your AI tasks. It chooses where each task runs, makes sure the task finishes, and keeps a log of the work. You keep full ownership of your data because the software runs on your own hardware. This prevents your private information from leaving your local computer or server.

## 🚀 First Time Run

After the installation finishes, you will see an icon on your desktop. Double-click the icon to launch the app. The first window you see is the setup dashboard. 

The software scans your hardware to check if your computer supports AI tasks. If your computer has a graphics card, the software identifies it to speed up performance. 

You need to set a local password for your account. This password ensures that only you see the logs and the data processed by the AI. Once you create this password, you reach the main screen.

## 🛠️ Configuring Your Agents

The main menu lists your active AI agents. You can add a new agent by clicking the "Add New" button. Provide a name for your agent and define its folder location. 

Annona acts as a gatekeeper. When an agent tries to perform an action, the software checks your rules. If an action falls outside your allowed region or policy, the software blocks it. This protects your data from being sent to unauthorized locations.

## 📋 Understanding the Logs

Every action recorded by the kernel appears in the "History" tab. You see the time, the task name, and the result. This gives you a clear record of what your agents do at all times. If a task fails, you see a red icon next to the entry. Click the entry to see a description of why the task stopped.

## 🛡️ Data Privacy

Annona does not upload your files to a cloud server. It performs all calculations on your machine. Because the software remains on your local disk, you satisfy strict privacy standards. You choose the specific folders the agents can touch. If you do not grant access to a folder, the agents cannot read or change those files.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

If the software fails to launch, verify that you have administrative rights on your Windows machine. Sometimes antivirus software blocks new applications. If this happens, add the annona installation folder to your antivirus whitelist. 

If your AI agents seem slow, check your memory usage in the Task Manager. Close other heavy applications to give the AI kernel enough power to finish its tasks. 

Ensure that you keep your version of Windows updated. Annona relies on modern Windows features to manage hardware resources efficiently. If you encounter bugs, check the help menu inside the app for a link to our community forum.
